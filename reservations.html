The book

We hold the table. You hold the time.

Requests are confirmed by email within a few hours during service days. Parties of seven or more go to the events desk. We ask 24 hours if you need to let the table go — the room is small and the fire does not scale.

Before you write

A few house rules.

Children are welcome at Sunday lunch. Dinner is for tables that can sit the length of the meal. The tasting is served to everyone at the table. Dogs stay outside on the terrace months. If you are late by twenty minutes, we give the table away — not out of spite, out of maths.

Prefer the phone? The desk is on +34 93 174 88 20 from 11:00, Tuesday to Saturday.
