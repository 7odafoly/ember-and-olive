The house

A small kitchen that still smells of the mill.

Lina Haddad cooks the eastern Mediterranean the way her grandmother pressed oil in Koura: slowly, with smoke in the room, and without apology for garlic. The fire is holm oak. The fish is on the counter before the lunch rush on the beach. The rest is salt, time, and a bottle you actually want to finish.

Visit

Walk from Santa Caterina. Don’t drive.

The door is a black timber frame on Carrer del Rec, between the old market and the water. We hold tables for 1 hour 45. If you are six or more, write the events desk — the Room cannot absorb a surprise.
